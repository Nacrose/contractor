## Contractor implementation implications

Implement draft capture, office review, confirmation/rejection, posting approval, attachment recovery and offline sync as distinct lifecycle steps. Keep the field outbox idempotent across a lost acknowledgment and process restart; expose terminal validation/permission failures for correction. Do not treat a queued field submission as a confirmed material receipt, expense posting or approved daily report. Contractor acceptance should require persistent local enqueue and restart recovery, even though the original web client treats IndexedDB write failure as best-effort. T01 still requires the remaining source workflows and detailed control mappings, while T04 must identify and verify the deployed backend contract.
